# Understanding the Concept of God



## Core Concept

The theological core of God rests on three interlocking attributes: **omniscience**, **omnipotence**, and **omnibenevolence**.  
- **Omniscience**: God’s all‑seeing knowledge encompasses past, present, and future, enabling perfect providence.  
- **Omnipotence**: God’s unlimited power guarantees that His will is always fulfilled, from creation to redemption.  
- **Omnibenevolence**: God’s perfect goodness ensures that His actions are always aimed at the ultimate good of His creation.  

True understanding moves beyond cataloguing these facts; as J.I. Packer notes, each truth must become a meditation that deepens personal communion with God, turning theoretical knowledge into lived experience.



## Practical Tips

- **Reflect daily**: Keep a journal where you record verses, insights, and concrete action plans. Review past entries to trace how God has worked in your life, as suggested by the Mighty Oaks Foundation.  
- **Seek diverse views**: Read Scripture from different translations and discuss passages with people of varied theological backgrounds. This broadens understanding and prevents echo‑chambers.
